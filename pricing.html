Travel & Location

How Geography Affects Pricing

Travel is built into every rate — no separate invoices for flights, hotels, or meals. Rates are based out of Pisgah Forest, NC. For engagements farther afield, a location adjustment applies.

Location Description Adjustment
Local Within ~2 hours driving of Pisgah Forest, NC — same-day, no overnight Included in base rate
Regional 2–4 hour drive — 1 overnight stay required + $800
National Flight required — 1 overnight stay + $1,500 – $1,800
Extended Travel 2 or more nights away from home + $700 per additional night

Virtual sessions carry no travel adjustment and are available to organizations anywhere in the world.

Booking & Cancellation

What to Expect

Every engagement is a partnership. These policies are designed to be fair to both parties and reflect the significant preparation that goes into every session.

📝 To Book
A signed agreement and 50% deposit are required to hold your date. Your date is not confirmed until both are received.
💳 Payment
The remaining 50% balance is due no later than the day of the engagement. Invoices are sent electronically and can be paid online by ACH bank transfer or credit card, or by check. Purchase orders are welcome.
📅 Cancellation by Client
The 50% deposit is non-refundable — it covers preparation work that begins at signing.

Balance refund schedule:
60+ days before event: full balance refunded
30–59 days: 50% of balance refunded
Under 30 days: no refund on balance
🔄 Rescheduling
Need to move the date? Rescheduling (rather than cancelling) is always preferred and can usually be accommodated with no penalty, subject to availability.
⚡ Cancellation by Vera
In the rare event Vera must cancel due to illness or emergency, you'll receive a full refund of all fees paid, plus priority rescheduling at the same rate.
